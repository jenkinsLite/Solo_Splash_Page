Attend a show or a concert

The Torshov theatre is a great place to spend your night out

Go for a picnic in the local park

The Torshov Park is perfect for a picnic, getting a tan, or just chilling out

Play sports at the Lilliborg field

Play volleyball, football, basketball of lift some weights
